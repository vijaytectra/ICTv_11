# ICT Reference & Concept Mapping Analysis

This document maps official Inner Circle Trader (ICT) concepts from institutional references to the current Python implementation, highlighting specific structural weaknesses and proposed quality improvements.

---

## Technical Mapping Matrix

| ICT Reference Concept | Current Implementation | Implementation Weakness | Quality Improvement | Backtest Validation Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HTF Directional Bias & Structure** | `add_master_htf_confluence()` uses 50-EMA vs 200-EMA on 5m chart | EMA crosses lag price action and do not represent institutional Order Flow or Market Structure (HH/HL, LH/LL) on 1H/15M. | Replace EMA with 1H Market Structure (Swings + BOS) and Daily/HTF FVG directional bias. | Measure OOS Win Rate improvement when signals align with 1H Market Structure vs 5m EMA. |
| **Premium vs. Discount Dealing Range** | Missing in signal generation routines | Trades are executed in Premium during BUYS or Discount during SELLS, leading to poor entry locations. | Calculate equilibrium (50% level) of active 1H/15M swing range. Buy ONLY in Discount ($<50\%$), Sell ONLY in Premium ($>50\%$). | Compare MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion) and Win Rate of Discount vs non-Discount entries. |
| **Liquidity Pools (PDH/PDL, Asian/London High/Low, EQH/EQL)** | `find_liquidity_sweeps()` looks at rolling 50-bar swing highs/lows | Sweeps any minor swing low instead of major institutional liquidity pools (Previous Day High/Low, Session Highs/Lows, Equal Highs/Lows). | Track explicit PDH/PDL, Asian Session High/Low, London Session High/Low, and Equal Highs/Lows ($< 1.5$ pips difference). | Filter signals by pool type swept; measure WR on PDH/PDL sweeps vs minor swing sweeps. |
| **Displacement & Energetic Expansion** | `find_fair_value_gaps()` uses fixed 2.0/2.5 pip gap size | Accepts small, sluggish FVGs formed during chop without strong institutional expansion or body displacement. | Require Displacement: Candle body ratio $> 60\%$, body size $\ge 1.8\times$ 20-bar ATR, and FVG gap size $\ge 2.5\times$ spread. | Evaluate setup win rate as a function of displacement ratio percentile. |
| **Market Structure Shift (MSS)** | Missing explicit MSS candle body break | Assumes a sweep immediately creates a setup without waiting for price to displace and close beyond the preceding swing point. | Require candle BODY close beyond the most recent opposing swing point (MSS) following a liquidity sweep. | Test setup WR with body close MSS vs wick sweep MSS. |
| **Unicorn Setup (Breaker + FVG)** | `generate_signals_setup_6()` checks `breaker_type == 1 and ftype == 1 and ob_t == 1` | Low frequency due to strict simultaneous requirement of 3 indicators on exact same candle $i$. | Allow Breaker Block + FVG overlap within a 3-bar window after MSS. Enforce entry at Breaker/FVG confluence zone. | Measure trade frequency and WR when allowing 3-bar confluence window vs single-bar matching. |
| **OB + FVG Confluence** | `generate_signals_setup_5()` checks overlapping top/bottom bounds | Uses simple average entry without checking if FVG is embedded inside or directly aligned with Order Block body. | Require FVG to originate from or overlap with Order Block body; entry at 50% FVG (Consequent Encroachment - CE). | Compare WR of embedded FVG+OB vs detached FVG+OB. |
| **ICT Silver Bullet** | `generate_signals_setup_3()` tags fixed time windows (10-11 AM, 3-4 AM EST) | Enters on any Silver Bullet FVG without requiring liquidity sweep prior to the hour or HTF alignment. | Require Silver Bullet window FVG to be preceded by liquidity sweep within the first 15 mins of the hour, aligned with HTF structure. | Run windowed backtest comparing filtered vs unfiltered Silver Bullet signals. |
